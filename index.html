About

I am a master's student in Economics and Computation at Duke University. I am interested in how recommender systems and generative AI shape consumer preferences and choices, and how firms and creators respond in what they offer and how they present it.

Before Duke, I earned a BBA in Applied Economics from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen.

Outside of research, I love swimming, music, and modern art.

Research interests

Substantive: Recommender systems, generative AI, consumer preferences, product differentiation, firm innovation.

Methods: Causal inference, machine learning, natural language processing, algorithm audits.
